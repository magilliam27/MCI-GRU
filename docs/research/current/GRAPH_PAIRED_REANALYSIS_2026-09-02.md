2. **The power table is the headline for Phase 1.** `sd(Δ)` at confirm is 0.038 (A1), 0.058 (A4), 0.075 (A3) and 0.089 (A2). 6. **The largest daily differences cluster on consecutive sessions** (2025-03-27/28/31, 05-13/14/15, 11-03/05, 12-16/17), which is the 5-session label overlap showing itself and is why the lag-4 HAC and the 5-session blocks are not optional.
7. **Sharpe error bars are 4.7–5.2 wide.** The annualised Newey–West Sharpe of the top-10 control book is 1.88 with a block-bootstrap interval of [−0.14, +4.55]; every arm's interval contains every other arm's point estimate at every K. The K-dependent "Sharpe reversal" in the ticket-167 report was noise, as the proposal suspected. **No paired daily portfolio return separates from the control at any K** (closest: A1 at K = 50, +0.00040 per day, p 0.098, BHY 0.82).
8. **Seed-paired per-model IC is the sharpest paired statistic in this report, and it points the same way.** Member i of every arm shares its initialisation seed with member i of the control. Over the twenty pairs, every arm's members are below the control's on mean daily IC (paired differences −0.0028 to −0.0080; paired t −1.8 to −3.2; only 4–5 of 20 pairs favour the arm). `[Inferred]` on one test year this is a consistent single-model deficit for every graph arm; it conditions on the same 238 days as the arbiter and so is not an independent year, and the ensemble narrows the gap because the arms with the weakest members gain the most from averaging (section 8).
9. **Per-model output scale varies by up to 200× within an arm** (CV 0.58–0.94), so the raw-mean ensemble is an implicit scale-weighted average. Re-ensembling by daily rank leaves the control first (0.0444) and moves top-K 20 to last (0.0343). The control's lead is not an artefact of scale weighting (section 9).

11. **The four graph-pipeline code facts are settled** and carried in section 10: top-K under `top_k_metric="corr"` ranks by signed correlation; `GATConv` self-loops carry mean-filled edge attributes, which is what makes A0 a two-layer MLP on node features and A1 the same computation for the names it isolates; `dropout_edge(force_undirected=False)` makes training graphs asymmetric and evaluation graphs symmetric; and the arbiter's label differs from the section-8 label.

`[Inferred]` **What this changes for the map, and what it does not.** It does not change the ticket-167 outcome-map branch; it removes the doubt that the branch was an artefact of comparing overlapping intervals as if independent. It also does not argue for a different arbiter: no secondary metric reverses the verdict, and the only statistic that is sharper than the arbiter (per-model pairing) agrees with it. What it does change is the cost of the next step: a four-fold multi-year design cannot resolve differences of the size seen in 2025 for any arm, so the Phase-1 grilling must either fix a minimum effect worth acting on that is at or above the MDEs in section 6, or plan more folds, or pre-register the "undecidable at this universe and horizon" branch as the likely landing. ### 2.2 Label and daily IC `[Verified]`

- **Arbiter label:** `close[t+5] / close[t+1] − 1`, computed by `realized_returns_from_market_data(market, label_t=5)`, which is the same formula as `mci_gru.data.preprocessing.compute_labels` (re-checked at `4803246`: both shift close by `−1` and by `−label_t` within each name and divide). This is what the production evaluation received as `true_returns`.
- **Section-8 label, alongside only:** `close[t+5] / close[t] − 1`, the variant the ablation notebook's per-year cell used.
- **Daily IC:** per test day, `cross_sectional_ic` over the names with a finite score and a finite label (at least two, non-degenerate), Pearson or Spearman, dated so that arms can be paired on the calendar. This is the same per-day rule as `daily_ic_series`.
- **Winsorised variant:** predictions clipped per day to that day's 1st and 99th percentiles before the Pearson IC.

### 2.3 Paired inference `[Verified]` as the code path, `[Inferred]` nowhere

For each stage and variant, the five daily series are inner-joined on date, `Δ_k(d) = IC_k(d) − IC_A0(d)` is formed for the four comparison arms, and for each:

- **Newey–West HAC** on the mean with Bartlett weights and **4 lags** (`label_t − 1`, the overlap-aware minimum the production code enforces); the p-value is the two-sided normal approximation `erfc(|t| / √2)`.
- **Circular moving-block percentile bootstrap** on the mean, **block 5** (`label_t`), 1,000 resamples, seed 1729, 95% level.
- **Benjamini–Hochberg–Yekutieli** step-up across the **four** arm-versus-control comparisons within a stage and variant (m = 4, c(4) = 2.083, monotone, capped at 1).
- `sd(Δ)` is the sample standard deviation over days; win rate is the share of days with Δ > 0.

### 2.4 Power `[Verified]` as arithmetic

Normal approximation: `MDE(n) = (z₀.₉₇₅ + z₀.₈₀) · sd(Δ) / √n = 2.8016 · sd(Δ) / √n`, two-sided 5%, 80% power; `days(MDE) = ⌈(2.8016 · sd / MDE)²⌉`. This treats pooled days as exchangeable draws with the 2025 `sd(Δ)`; section 13 says what that leaves out.

### 2.5 Portfolios and Sharpe `[Verified]`

Equal-weight top-K basket by ensemble score each day, K ∈ {10, 20, 50, 100}, realised on the arbiter label (`top_k_returns`), so each day's "return" is the basket's 5-session forward return and consecutive days overlap. Sharpe is the annualised Newey–West Sharpe (4 lags) with a circular block bootstrap **of the Sharpe itself** (block 5, 1,000 resamples, seed 1729 + K). The daily basket returns are then paired against the control's with the same HAC / block / BHY machinery, BHY within each K.

### 2.6 Seed pairing and the scale audit `[Verified]`

`train_multiple_models` seeds member *i* with `config.seed + i` (re-checked at `4803246`), and every arm ran with `seed=1729`, so member *i* of arm *k* and member *i* of A0 share initialisation. For each of the 20 members: mean daily Pearson IC on the arbiter label, and the mean over days of the cross-sectional standard deviation of its scores. The rank-averaged ensemble ranks each member's scores within each day, averages the ranks over the 20 members, and re-scores that surface with the same daily IC. The proposal's stated paired-t over seeds is reported as the notebook computed it; its p-values were not part of the artifact and are derived in section 8 with Student's t on 19 degrees of freedom.

## 10. Code facts `[Verified]`

Settled during the build of this ticket's module and recorded on pull request 180; the first and fourth re-checked against `mci_gru/graph/correlation.py` and `mci_gru/data/preprocessing.py` at `4803246` for this report, the second and third established by running `torch_geometric` 2.7.0 in the build session.

1. **Top-K ranks by signed correlation under the arm's setting.** `_select_edges_topk` applies `np.abs` to the score only when `top_k_metric == "abs_corr"` (`correlation.py:237-238`); with `"corr"`, which A3 used, it ranks by the signed value, and the kept `corr` channel is always signed. The ticket-167 report's worry that top-K might have been selecting on |corr| is closed: it selected the K most positively correlated names.
2. **Self-loops carry mean-filled edge attributes, which is what makes the control a two-layer MLP and the incumbent the same computation for the names it isolates.** `GATConv` in `torch_geometric` 2.7.0 defaults to `add_self_loops=True, fill_value="mean"` and routes `edge_attr` through `lin_edge` into the attention logits. On an empty graph every node gets a self-loop whose attributes are the mean of nothing, which is zero, so A0's graph stream reduces to a node-wise transform of node features. A node with one incoming edge inherits that edge's attributes on its self-loop; a node with none gets zeros — so for the 75–78% of admissible names A1 isolates (ticket-167 report, section 6) A1 and A0 perform the same computation. This is the mechanism behind A1's near-zero paired difference, the K = 10 identical baskets in section 7.2, and the member-level pairing floor in section 4.1.
3. **Training graphs are asymmetric; evaluation graphs are symmetric.** `dropout_edge(force_undirected=False, p=0.1)`, the trunk's training-time regulariser, drops directed edges independently: on a symmetric 60-node test graph 10.2% of the kept directed edges lost their reverse, while `force_undirected=True` left none. Evaluation skips dropout, so the model is trained on graphs of a different shape from the ones it is scored on. Not measured here for its effect on IC; a deferred ticket in the proposal.
4. **Two labels are in circulation.** The arbiter's is `close[t+5] / close[t+1] − 1` (`compute_labels`; `realized_returns_from_market_data` is the same formula). The ablation notebook's per-year cell used `close[t+5] / close[t] − 1`. Section 3 quantifies the difference and shows it is the whole gap in the ticket-167 report's per-year table.

**On the ensembling rule (proposal §3.3).** The raw mean is scale-weighted and rank averaging changes the ordering of the populated arms but not the control's place. Reporting the rank-averaged arbiter alongside costs nothing; adopting it is a recipe change and out of scope.

**On the positive control (proposal §2).** Δ(A1) is at zero as the proposal expected; its interval is not tight, for the reason above. ## 13. Limits

- **One test year, 238 days.** Every statistic here is conditional on 2025. The power table extrapolates the 2025 `sd(Δ)` to pooled days as if days were exchangeable across years; a year with a different regime, or a rolling five-year training window instead of the expanding eight-year one the 2025 run used, can move `sd(Δ)` in either direction. The MDEs are the best available estimate, not a measurement of a multi-year design.
- **The power arithmetic is the normal approximation** and ignores the residual autocorrelation the HAC and blocks correct for within a year; treat the day counts as lower bounds.
- **The seed-paired test is on one year's days**, and its p-values were derived from the artifact's t-statistics after the fact (section 8), not pre-registered.
- **BHY is applied within stage and variant** with m = 4. Across the sixteen arm-variant comparisons at confirm, or across the two stages, no further correction is applied; every raw p is ≥ 0.29, so none would change a verdict.
- **Basket returns are unhedged 5-session forward returns of the top-K names**, not the economic replay in `mci_gru/evaluation/backtest_engine.py`; no costs, no benchmark. They are used only to put an error bar under the ticket-167 report's Sharpe figures.
- **Nothing requiring a checkpoint forward pass was done** — no edge permutation, no edge zeroing at inference, no attribution. Those are the proposal's Phase 6.
- **The contract tests were not run in Colab** and are not cited as run evidence; the run evidence is section 2.7 and the Drive artifacts.
